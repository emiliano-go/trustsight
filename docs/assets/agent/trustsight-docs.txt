configure it, what a rule or report means, or what the project's own
measurements say.

## Rules for answering

- Do not describe TrustSight as a scanner that declares packages safe. It
  reports evidence and coverage; a person decides.
- `UNFLAGGED` means the score stayed at or below 20. `INCONCLUSIVE` means the
  analysis could not read enough. Never present either as a safety verdict.
- Quote a rule id with its category page when the question is about a specific
  finding.
- For live AUR alerts, use <https://watcher.trustsight.org/api>.
